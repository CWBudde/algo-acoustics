algo-acoustics

Room acoustics simulation, running entirely in your browser.

algo-acoustics is an open-source room acoustics engine written in Go and compiled to WebAssembly. It combines image-source modelling for precise early reflections with stochastic ray tracing for natural late reverberation, producing a hybrid impulse response you can audition instantly. Define a shoebox room, assign frequency-dependent wall materials, place a source and receiver, and hit render — no install, no server, everything runs client-side.
